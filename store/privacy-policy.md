# Pezzadox Worlds privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Pezzadox Worlds collects nothing about you.

## What the app does on your phone

It downloads the latest episode list and pages from www.pezzadox.com and keeps a copy on your phone so it works offline. It remembers which episodes you've opened, so it can mark new ones, and whether you turned on new episode alerts. All of this stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

## New episode alerts

If you turn them on, your phone checks www.pezzadox.com every few hours and shows a notification when a new episode appears. The check sends nothing about you, and the notification is created on your phone. You can turn alerts off in the app or in your phone's settings.

## What is never collected

* No account, name, email address or phone number.
* No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
* No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.

## Other sites

Episodes play on YouTube, and links open other sites such as Instagram and TikTok. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Pages and games from www.pezzadox.com open inside the app.

## Children

The app is rated for everyone and collects no data from anyone.
