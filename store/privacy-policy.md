# Pezzadox Worlds privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Pezzadox Worlds collects nothing about you unless you ask Kyle for a video message. If you do, it keeps only what is needed to make and deliver that video, as set out below.

## What the app does on your phone

It downloads the latest episode list and pages from www.pezzadox.com and keeps a copy on your phone so it works offline. It remembers which episodes you've opened, so it can mark new ones, and whether you turned on new episode alerts. All of this stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

## New episode alerts

If you turn them on, your phone checks www.pezzadox.com every few hours and shows a notification when a new episode appears. The check sends nothing about you, and the notification is created on your phone. You can turn alerts off in the app or in your phone's settings.

## Video messages (Android)

You can pay for a personal video message from Kyle. This is the only part of the app that sends anything about you to us.

**What is collected, and why**

* The name of the person the video is for, the name it is from, the occasion, and what you ask Kyle to say. Kyle reads these to record the video.
* Your Google Play purchase token and order number for that payment, so we can confirm it with Google and refund it. Google Play takes the payment; we never see your card or your Google account details.
* A random code created on your phone. It is how the app shows you your own requests and nobody else's. It is not linked to your name, email or device identifiers.
* The finished video.

**Where it goes.** It is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and the payment is checked with Google Play. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

**How long it is kept.** Until you delete it. In the app, open Video messages and tap Delete on a request: that removes the request and its video straight away. A request still waiting for Kyle can be cancelled there for a full refund, and then deleted. Unpaid, unfinished requests are removed automatically after a day. We keep the payment records that the law requires for tax and accounting. To have everything removed without the app, email the address below.

**Refunds.** If the video is not ready within 14 days, or Kyle declines the request, you are refunded in full automatically. You can cancel for a full refund at any time before the video is recorded.

**Sharing.** If you tap "Send to", the app makes a private link to the video that works for 7 days, and you choose who to send it to.

**Children.** Video messages must be bought by an adult. Do not put a child's full name, school or address in a request.

Our lawful basis under UK data protection law is that this is needed to provide what you paid for. You have the right to see, correct or delete this information and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## What is never collected

* No account, email address or phone number. No names, apart from the first names you type into a video message request.
* No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
* No advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking.

## Other sites

Episodes play on YouTube, and links open other sites such as Instagram and TikTok. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Pages and games from www.pezzadox.com open inside the app.

## Children

The app is rated for everyone. Browsing, watching and playing collect no data from anyone. Video messages are for adults to buy.
